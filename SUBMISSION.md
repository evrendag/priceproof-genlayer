# Portal submission
## Project name
PRICEPROOF
## One-line summary
A consensus court that verifies retail price and discount claims against live official sources and issues challengeable on-chain receipts.
## Description (under 1000 characters)
PRICEPROOF is a reusable GenLayer protocol for verifying retail price and discount claims against live official sources. A merchant or consumer submits structured offer data, product and terms URLs, displayed prices, discount rate, scope and validity. The Intelligent Contract fetches those pages and validators independently decide whether the evidence supports the claim. Verdict, primary gap, currency and material facts must match exactly; scores may differ by at most 10 points within the same band. Deterministic checks reject malformed URLs, impossible values, invalid transitions and stale versions. VERIFIED claims become ACTIVE receipts; qualified claims expose limitations; misleading claims are REJECTED; unavailable or conflicting evidence fails closed to MANUAL_REVIEW. Receipts can be challenged with new official evidence and are superseded through explicit lineage. The app manages wallet submission, audit status and receipts end to end.
